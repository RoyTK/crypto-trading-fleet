Qualification criteria (a wallet QUALIFIES if all are true)

Read all of these from the wallet-analyzer page (NOT the per-token table). The Realized-vs-Unrealized PnL split is the single most important thing — it tells you whether the wallet actually EXITS (which is the bot's whole edge) or just holds bags.

REALIZED PnL is significantly positive. Use REALIZED, not Total PnL. Total = Realized + Unrealized, and a big Total can be mostly paper gains on still-held bags. Realized = profits actually banked = exit skill, which is exactly what the bot copies.
Unrealized PnL is NOT deeply negative. REJECT if unrealized losses exceed realized gains — that's a bag-holder who enters good tokens but rides them to zero instead of exiting (the anti-pattern). Small or positive unrealized is fine.
Avg Holding Duration in the FOLLOWABLE window ~30 minutes to 7 days. The bot's mechanism is a ~15-minute co-buy cluster with fast ladder/pullback exits. TWO failure modes: TOO SLOW (~14-27+ day swing) is the wrong style; and TOO FAST (sub-~15-min holds — 7-second / 1-minute snipers) is UNFOLLOWABLE — by the time COPY detects the buy + forms the cluster + executes (~10s to a few minutes), the wallet has already sold, so COPY buys its exit. An otherwise-great wallet that holds sub-15-min is NOT a qualifier for COPY (note it separately as "TOO FAST — future fast bot"); don't add it to the list as a normal candidate.
Multiple multi-x wins (count of >100% and >500% in the PnL distribution donut). Several multi-x wins beats one big win.
Pattern of entering early and exiting with multi-x profits on small caps / new mints ($200 profit on $400 counts).
NOT brand new — exclude wallets only a few days old (roughly 1 week minimum; 1+ month preferred).
Classified as Smart Money by Birdeye.

IMPORTANT — the "Scam/Rug tokens %" metric is NOT a disqualifier; a HIGH value is expected and even confirmatory for the wallets we want. The bot's edge is riding fresh-mint pumps (most of which later rug) and exiting before the collapse, so the ideal wallet trades LOTS of rug-prone tokens AND still has strongly positive REALIZED PnL, small unrealized losses, and short holds.
  - High Scam/Rug % + positive Realized + low Unrealized + short hold = EXACTLY what we want. Top pick.
  - High Scam/Rug % + big Unrealized losses + long hold = a bag-holder. Reject.
(Worked example: a wallet with Realized +$38.9K / Unrealized +$9.8K / 7d hold / 73% rug is ideal; a wallet with Realized +$95K but Unrealized −$241K / 27d hold / 93% rug is a REJECT despite the big realized number — it holds rug bags to zero.)

Exclude / ignore

Exclude: brand-new wallets, pure market-maker/HFT bots, wallets with negative REALIZED PnL, and BAG-HOLDERS (unrealized losses bigger than realized gains, and/or avg holding duration ~14+ days). Log excluded wallets in a separate EXCLUDED section for reference, but don't treat them as candidates.
Ignore win rate entirely (lots of small losses are fine — positive skew is the norm here).
Ignore SOL in PnL calculations.
Do NOT exclude a wallet for a high Scam/Rug % alone — for this strategy that's a feature, not a bug, as long as the realized/unrealized/hold criteria above hold.

De-duplication (ONE ROW PER WALLET, append-only)

Before adding a wallet, read SOL_early_token_traders.txt and collect every FULL address already present. Only append wallets whose address is NOT already in the file. Do NOT add "UPDATED" rows, and do NOT edit or re-add existing wallets — if a wallet is already listed, skip it entirely. One row per wallet, forever.
Why: an automated ingestion script reads this file into the bot's wallet pool and de-dupes by address, so duplicate or "UPDATED" rows are ignored anyway. And lifetime-PnL changes on an already-listed wallet don't matter — once ingested, the bot scores each wallet by its LIVE cluster behavior (does it co-buy winning tokens with other tracked wallets?), not by the curated PnL number. So PnL is only a coarse entry filter here, not the thing that earns a wallet its place. Keep the bar where the prompt already sets it; don't tighten it into a high PnL floor (big-PnL wallets are often funds/treasuries that don't generate usable signals).
