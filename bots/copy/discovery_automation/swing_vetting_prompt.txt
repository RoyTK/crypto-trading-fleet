SWING-COPY WALLET VETTING (browser Claude) — created 2026-10-03
===============================================================

WHAT SWING DOES
---------------
Swing follows ONE wallet INTO a token when that wallet NET-buys >= $500 (buys minus sells) and
follows it OUT when the wallet has net-sold >= 50% of what it built (small "suppression" sells
are held through). Hold horizon: hours to days. Our latency (7s – 2 min) is noise on that
horizon — so the wallet we want BUILDS a position over hours/days and SELLS IT DOWN GRADUALLY.
A wallet that flips in minutes, market-makes, or is one of several wallets run by the same bot
loses us money (live data: market-maker/bot wallets caused most of swing's losses).

THIS RUN IS BLIND
-----------------
Do NOT look up our own trading results for these wallets (no Grafana, no DB). Judge only what
Birdeye/Solscan show. Your verdicts are compared afterwards with our actual P&L to test the
vetting itself.

DATA SOURCES (Birdeye, no login)
--------------------------------
PRIMARY = direct-fetch the internal `forge` endpoints (credentials:include). This
vetted 310 wallets across two runs with ZERO errors and no UI scraping — use it:
  - periodic (realized/unrealized PnL, the >500%/>100% distribution bands, rug%):
      GET https://birdeye.so/forge/solana/wallet-pnl/periodic?wallet=<ADDR>&periodic=
      NOTE: the distribution bands are TOKEN COUNTS, not %. Compute
      pct_ge_100 = (gt_500 + 100_500) / sum(all bands). Bands are mutually exclusive.
  - token-traded (per-token liquidity flag for followability, buy counts for
    accumulation, first->last trade span for hold-shape):
      GET https://birdeye.so/forge/solana/wallet-pnl/token-traded?wallet=<ADDR>&order_field=volume_usd&order_type=desc&offset=0&limit=100&hide=
      From this derive: liqShare (% of tokens that are liquid/followable), liqPnl
      (% of the wallet's PnL that comes from those followable tokens), medBuys
      (median buys/token = accumulation depth), nTok (sample size), and hold_shape
      (bin each token's last_trade-first_trade span into <1m/1-5m/5-15m/15-60m/
      1-6h/6-24h/1-7d/>7d; "mid" = the 5m-24h mass).
  - holding-duration (per-trade durations in seconds; finer hold view, recent
    sample only): POST https://birdeye.so/forge/solana/wallet-pnl/holding-duration
    (wallet in POST body; GET 404s). Optional — token-traded spans are enough.
FALLBACK / spot-check = the UI page https://birdeye.so/solana/wallet-analyzer/<ADDRESS>
(timeframe = ALL): WIN RATE, PNL DISTRIBUTION, ANALYTICS (Avg Hold, Txs), SUSPICIOUS
ACTIONS, and the "Hide Low Liq/Scam" toggle on TOKENS TRADED.

  Always pull FULL addresses from the href, never truncated on-screen text. Everything

NOTE: `instant_sell` is NOT exposed by the forge endpoints (only the UI Suspicious-

From token-traded also derive medSells (median sells per token). Note Txs per day from the

Bot-farm check (Solscan, https://solscan.io/account/<ADDR>): who funded the wallet (first

VETTING CRITERIA
----------------
KEEP_SWING only if ALL hold:
  [ ] Hold shape: (6-24h + 1-7d) mass >= ~35% of tokens, AND <1m < ~30%, AND >7d not a
      majority (>~60% >7d = parked bags, not swings).
  [ ] Builds AND unwinds: medBuys >= ~2 and medSells >= ~2 (accumulates, then sells down in
      pieces — that is what our net-flow entry/exit follows). medSells = 1 dumps in one go:
      fine only if hold shape is clearly multi-hour.
  [ ] Not a market-maker/HFT bot: Txs/day < ~200 and tokens traded per week < ~150.
  [ ] Followable profit: liqPnl >= ~50%.
  [ ] Banked track record: realized PnL >= ~$5-10k on nTok >= ~10.
  [ ] Not a bundle: no shared funder / mirrored activity with another wallet in this list.
REJECT (note which):
  - mostly <1m–15m holds                        -> TOO_FAST
  - thousands of txs, hundreds of tokens/week   -> MM_BOT
  - shares a funder / mirrors another wallet    -> BUNDLE  (name the partner)
  - big unrealized bag vs realized              -> BAGHOLDER
  - profit mostly from illiquid micro-mints     -> UNFOLLOWABLE_THIN
  - passes shape but thin realized / nTok < 10  -> LOW_SAMPLE
  - can't decide                                -> UNCERTAIN (say why)
