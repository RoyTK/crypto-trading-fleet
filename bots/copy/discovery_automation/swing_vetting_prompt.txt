WHAT SWING DOES
---------------
Swing follows ONE wallet INTO a token when that wallet NET-buys >= $500 (buys minus sells) and
follows it OUT when the wallet has net-sold >= 50% of what it built (small "suppression" sells
are held through). Hold horizon: hours to days. Our latency (7s – 2 min) is noise on that
horizon — so the wallet we want BUILDS a position over hours/days and SELLS IT DOWN GRADUALLY.
A wallet that flips in minutes, market-makes, or is one of several wallets run by the same bot
loses us money (live data: market-maker/bot wallets caused most of swing's losses).

VETTING CRITERIA
----------------
KEEP_SWING only if ALL hold:
  [ ] Hold shape: (6-24h + 1-7d) mass >= ~35% of tokens, AND <1m < ~30%, AND >7d not a
      majority (>~60% >7d = parked bags, not swings).
  [ ] Builds AND unwinds: medBuys >= ~2 and medSells >= ~2 (accumulates, then sells down in
      pieces — that is what our net-flow entry/exit follows). medSells = 1 dumps in one go:
      fine only if hold shape is clearly multi-hour.
  [ ] Not a market-maker/HFT bot: Txs/day < ~200 and tokens traded per week < ~150.
  [ ] Followable profit: liqPnl >= ~50%.
  [ ] Banked track record: realized PnL >= ~$5-10k on nTok >= ~10.
  [ ] Not a bundle: no shared funder / mirrored activity with another wallet in this list.
REJECT (note which):
