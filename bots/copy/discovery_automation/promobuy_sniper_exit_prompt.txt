WHY THIS EXISTS
---------------
We run a paper strategy "promo-buy": it buys Solana memecoins that just got a PAID
Dexscreener promotion (a boost). Most rug within ~1-2h; a minority run 2x-35x. Our own
database only sees the wallets WE subscribe to — it CANNOT see the actual early snipers
that pile into these tokens at launch. Roy's thesis: **we can't follow snipers IN (too
fast, adverse selection), but their EXIT behavior may tell us WHEN TO GET OUT, and
certain snipers may recur across promoted tokens and mark quality / trajectory.**

Three questions to answer with data:
1. RECURRING SNIPERS — do specific wallet addresses show up as early buyers across
   MANY promoted tokens? (Systematic promo-snipers.)
2. EXIT-AS-SIGNAL — when a recurring sniper SELLS, does the token dump shortly after?
   i.e. is a specific sniper's exit a leading indicator we could "follow out" on?
3. FAST-RUG vs RUN PROFILE — is there an early tell (in the first ~5-15 min after
   launch/promo) that separates the tokens that RAN from the ones that fast-rugged?
   Candidates: fraction of early buyers who dump in the first minutes; top-holder /
   bundler concentration; holder-count trajectory; dev/creator behavior.

PER-TOKEN METHOD (Birdeye, no login)
------------------------------------
For each mint, open the Birdeye token / wallet data. Useful surfaces (any that load):
 - Token page: https://birdeye.so/token/<MINT>?chain=solana  (Trades / Traders / Holders
   / Top Traders tabs). Look at the EARLIEST trades and the top early buyers.
 - Per-wallet: https://birdeye.so/solana/wallet-analyzer/<WALLET>  (that wallet's win
   rate, avg holding duration, instant-sell %, scam/rug %, tokens traded) — use to
   profile a recurring sniper.
 - Internal forge endpoints (no login/key, faster; re-derive from the Network tab if a
   path 404s): token-traded, holding-duration, wallet-pnl/periodic (see the team's
   reference_birdeye_endpoints notes if available).
Capture, per token:
 - The 10-30 EARLIEST buyer wallets + their buy time (seconds/minutes after the token
   first traded).
 - For each early buyer: did they SELL, and roughly WHEN relative to the token's peak
   (before the top? at the top? never / still holding?).
 - How many of the early buyers dumped within the first ~5, ~10, ~15 minutes.
 - Top-holder concentration and holder count early on, if visible.
